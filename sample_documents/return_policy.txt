# Company Return and Refund Policy

## 1. Return Eligibility
Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery. Products must be unused, undamaged, and returned with their original packaging.

## 2. Non-Returnable Products
Personalized products, perishable goods, and products marked as non-returnable cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## 3. Return Process
Customers can request a return through the customer support portal or by contacting customer service with their order ID and reason for return.

## 4. Refund Processing
Once the returned product is received and inspected, approved refunds are initiated within 5–7 business days. The time taken for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider.

## 5. Damaged or Defective Products
Customers must report damaged or defective products within 48 hours of delivery and provide supporting photographs.

## 6. Return Shipping
Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or incorrectly delivered products. For other eligible returns, customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

## 7. Exchange Policy
Eligible products can be exchanged subject to stock availability. Customers should contact customer support to initiate an exchange.
