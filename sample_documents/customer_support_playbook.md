# Customer Support Playbook

## Delivery delays
Acknowledge the inconvenience, check carrier tracking, and provide the latest verified delivery estimate. Escalate orders delayed more than five business days.

## Refund requests
Confirm return eligibility and inspection status. Do not promise a refund date before approval. Approved refunds normally take 5–7 business days.

## Product quality
Apologize, collect order details and evidence, and route defective-product cases to the quality team. Repeated defects in one product category should be escalated to operations.

## Payment issues
Never ask customers to share full card details, PINs, or passwords. Verify payment status through the payment provider's transaction reference.
